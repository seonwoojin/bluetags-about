Alerts

Alerts tell you when a project you follow posts an announcement or has an event coming up.

Which projects send alerts

Only projects on your Watchlist. Add a project with the star on its page; remove it the same way.

What you get

  • New announcements from the project's official channels
  • A reminder the day before a mint, launch or AMA on the Calendar

Where alerts arrive

Alerts appear on your Watchlist with a dot next to the project. Email alerts can be turned on in your account settings after you sign in.

Too many alerts?

Remove projects you no longer follow from your Watchlist. There is no separate mute switch yet.
