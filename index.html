Getting started

Bluetags keeps track of the projects you care about — NFT collections, Layer-1 and Layer-2 networks — and brings their news, schedules and community talk into one place.

1. Find projects

Open Project to browse by category. Each project page shows its links, upcoming events and the latest posts from its community.

2. Build a watchlist

Sign in and press the star on a project page to add it to your Watchlist. The watchlist shows only the projects you follow, newest updates first.

3. Keep up with events

The Calendar lists mints, launches and AMAs for every project. Projects on your watchlist are highlighted.

4. Get alerts

Want announcements to come to you instead? See Alerts for how notifications work and how to choose which projects send them.

FAQ

Is Bluetags free?

Yes. Browsing, the watchlist and alerts are free.

Do I need a wallet?

No. You can sign in with Google, Kakao or email.

A project I follow is missing.

Ask for it in Community and we will add it.
